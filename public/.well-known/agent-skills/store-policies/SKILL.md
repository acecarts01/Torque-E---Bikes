# Store policies

- Ships Australia-wide. Lithium batteries are Class 9 dangerous goods and are shipped by certified carriers.
- Shipping is 10% of the order total, free for orders from 9900 AUD.
- Prices include 10% GST.
- Payment: bank-transfer, payid, crypto-BTC, crypto-USDT. Payment details are sent after the order is placed.
- Bikes above 250W are for off-road and private property use only.
- Full policies: `https://torquee-bikesaustralia.com.au/shipping/`, `https://torquee-bikesaustralia.com.au/faq/`. MCP tool: `get_policies`.
