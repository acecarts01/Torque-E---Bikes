## Phase 1: baseline (all 629 sitemap URLs crawled)
Good: all 200, none noindex, every canonical correct, titles and descriptions all unique, one H1 everywhere, no orphan pages (max click depth 4), raw HTML equals rendered HTML, Googlebot view identical, real 404s with noindex, no JSON-LD errors, CLS 0.
