4. Options for the 372 product pages with no model keyword

  1. Use fewer keywords on those pages (recommended for accessories and obscure models): keep the honest 1 primary; do not invent secondaries.
  2. Fill them with type-level Semrush keywords (for example "kids electric dirt bike for sale", "electric dirt bike for teenagers"), each used on exactly one product page, so every keyword is real Semrush data and still unique. The category pulls above supply thousands of these.
  3. Merge variant pages (colour/size variants of one model) so one page carries the keywords; flagged earlier as a cannibalisation risk.
