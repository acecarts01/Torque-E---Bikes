# Competitor notes for new blog posts (28 Oct 2026)

Method: for each focus keyword, search the top results, open the leading pages and record strengths and weaknesses. Posts are then planned to beat them on information gain (rule 8 of the blog-post skill). Notes are recorded before writing.

## fastest e bike (pages: eozzie.com.au, ecoflow.com/au, punkride.com)
Strengths: keyword-led H2s, wattage table, state-by-state summaries, short FAQ, contents list (EcoFlow).
Weaknesses: no official sources; contradictory legal claims (throttle, 200W, "same limit in all states"); contradictory speed figures and unsupported claims (120 km/h); no prices, range or battery data; no helmet or safety gear advice; missing ACT/NT; US "Class 3" terms in an Australian article; stale dates; promotional tone.
Our plan: cite Queensland and Transport for NSW (250W continuous, assist to 25 km/h, throttle only to 6 km/h, NSW 500W to 250W on 1 March 2029, seizure laws from 21 Aug 2026); table of legal e-bike vs private-property bikes with our real prices and no invented top speeds; what actually limits speed; safety gear (AS/NZS 1698 helmets, AS 1609 goggles); where fast bikes can be ridden.

## ebike accessories (pages: electrickicks.com.au, eozzie.com.au, pushys, canyon)
Strengths: category grouping, many product links, contents list (Electric Kicks), 11 accessory types (E-Ozzie).
Weaknesses: no prices or comparison table; no named standards or official sources; no sizing, weight limit or compatibility detail; no install or maintenance advice; stale dates; e-scooter items mixed in; mismatched or scooter URLs; thin sections (baskets, bottles, tool kits); typos.
Our plan: one hub with an AUD price table of accessories we actually stock, each linked to a full guide on this site (helmet, lights, lock, alarm, panniers, basket, mirror, phone mount, bottle, gloves, cover, pedals, seat cover, trailer, child seat), named standards (AS/NZS 2063), Queensland equipment rules, what to buy first.

## electric bikes for sale / buy electric bike (pages: 99bikes.com.au, rideelectric.com.au, dirodi.com.au, bairbikes.com)
Strengths: clear category structure, FAQ on range/charging/laws, links to all eight state rule pages (99 Bikes).
Weaknesses: no buying checklist (Wh, torque, weight, frame size, warranty); no AUD price bands or comparison table; few named models; shallow battery and ownership content; no sizing or test-ride advice; mis-pointed category links and visible template errors; no updated date.
Our plan: price bands from our own catalogue with named models, comparison table by type, battery watt-hour explanation and replacement price examples from our range, sizing and fit, consumer guarantees (ACCC), Queensland and NSW rules in plain English, how buying from us works (Australia-wide DG-certified shipping, payment methods).
