## New pages proposed
- **Electric Bikes hub (/electric-bikes/)** (Category gap): primary ebike (33,100/KD 17); cluster demand 53,500. The biggest umbrella searches ("ebike", "e bike electric") have no single page. The catalogue is split across 8 bike categories, so none of them can own the umbrella term.
- **Budget Electric Bikes (/electric-bikes/budget/)** (Category gap): primary low cost electric bicycle (3,600/KD 17); cluster demand 17,900. Price-led searches ("cheap", "affordable", "under $1000") are a distinct buying intent. A filtered page listing the lowest-priced bikes captures it without a new product line.
- **Brisbane local page (/electric-bikes/brisbane/)** (Category gap (conditional)): primary electric bikes brisbane (2,400/KD 17); cluster demand 3,290. Only build if there is a real customer-facing Brisbane location or pickup point; the site currently shows a postcode-level address only.

## Link-magnet candidates
- **E-bike conversion kit finder and legal-status checker** (Free tool) — People searching "conversion kit" and "ebike conversion kit" ask the same decision questions: which motor, which battery, will it fit my bike, and is it road legal or off-road only. A short tool that answers them is genuinely useful and gets linked to by forums and bike clubs. Signal: "ebike conversion kit" (6,600 searches) plus "conversion kit" (5,400) at very low difficulty (KD 3–6). Format: Four inputs (bike type, rider weight, terrain, road or off-road) returning a kit suggestion from the catalogue and a plain-English legal note. Static page with a small script; no accounts.
- **Australian e-bike rules by state: official sources hub** (Resource hub) — E-bike law searches are numerous and people want one trusted place that links straight to each regulator. A hub with plain-English summaries and links to Transport for NSW, VicRoads, Queensland TMR and the others becomes the page others cite. Signal: The e-bike law cluster totals about 4,390 monthly searches across 88 keyword variants. Format: State-by-state table with last-checked dates, written only from the regulators’ own pages, with the off-road-only framing for high-power bikes. Needs a review date and an owner.
