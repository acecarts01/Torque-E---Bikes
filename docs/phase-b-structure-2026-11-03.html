5. How Phases C, D and E use the bank

  1. C. Mapping proposal (you approve before anything is inserted): per page, current keywords beside proposed ones; each removed generated keyword beside its Semrush replacement; the 10 pages with no keywords; the 147 KD breaches fixed.
  2. D. Insert and resubmit: update the register and seo.generated.json, then titles, H1s, metas, alts and internal-link anchors. Build, crosscheck, push, wait for Vercel "Deployment has completed", check live, then resubmit sitemaps (Google, Bing), IndexNow and request indexing for the top URLs.
  3. E. Verify: re-check live pages and the Search Console notification panel; confirm every indexing issue is fixed or validated.

6. Fixed rules for every phase

  • Semrush data only: every generated keyword is removed and replaced with a Semrush one.
  • Products, categories, brands: Transactional or Commercial only, Transactional first. Blogs: main + 5 secondaries T/C, plus 10 unique tags.
  • KD 28 or lower, real volume above 0, one keyword on one page, no GST wording, nothing over 250W described as road legal.
  • Reviews are never touched. Every phase ends with a zero-error double check and your approval.
