## 6. What this means

- **Off-page is the biggest gap.** The site has no backlinks, no referring domains and no AI mentions, so almost nothing ranks above position 50. Fixing keywords alone will not move this.
- **Bing has indexed nothing yet** (679 discovered). Resubmitting the current sitemap (615 URLs, thin pages removed) is part of the insert phase.
- **Products are the long tail:** 367 of 446 products are not yet confirmed indexed, most of them "discovered, not indexed" (Google knows the URL but has not crawled it).
- **Merged pages still ranked under their old URLs**, for example /blog/best-electric-dirt-bikes-in-australia/. These now redirect (308) to /blog/electric-vs-petrol-dirt-bikes/, so their rankings should transfer once Google recrawls.
- **Limits:** the Semrush export covers one device and one country database, and Google's indexing report is dated 23 Oct.
