2. The Two-Way Gap Harvest rule (as saved)

#Rule
1Keywords with no page go on a keyword-gap list (volume, KD, intent, proposed page), ready for when you add products or pages.
2Products or brands with demand that we do not stock go on a product-gap list; catalogue products below 1 primary + 5 secondaries go on a thin-product list with the reason.
3Never say "no Semrush data" from a filtered pull. Run unfiltered first and report why results were dropped: KD above 28, Informational or Navigational only, KD n/a, low volume. Search the way people type.
4No merging pages and no padding with generic keywords to hide a shortfall. Type-level keywords only with your approval, one keyword on one page.
5Both directions on every run, every session, every project.
6Each session delivers keyword-gaps.csv, product-gaps.csv, thin-products.csv and no-demand-products.csv/.html.
7No-Demand Products Report for you to decide: remove, consolidate or keep. I never act before your explicit approval.

4. How a product ends up in that report

  1. Search "brand + distinctive model", then the model alone, then the product-type wording, each with no filters.
  2. Classify every result: usable, KD above 28, Informational or Navigational only, KD n/a (refresh it), below the volume floor.
  3. Products with data that only fail a filter go to the thin-product list (not for removal).
  4. Only products with nothing usable after all three searches reach the no-demand report.

5. What happens after you decide

  • Nothing is removed, merged, redirected or noindexed until you approve, product by product or group by group.
  • Approved removals get 301 redirects to the nearest relevant live page, and the sitemap, internal links, llms.txt and agent files are updated.
  • The build and crosscheck must pass, and the Vercel deploy must show complete, before any live check.
